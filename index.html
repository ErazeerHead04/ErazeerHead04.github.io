Social Machines & Robotics (SMART) Lab, NYU Abu Dhabi

Supportive but Truthful

Senior capstone on sycophancy in emotional-support AI

Research Assistant
PI: Prof. Dr. Hanan Salam
Jan 2026 to present

Emotional-support chatbots are often trained with reinforcement learning to improve a user's mood. I show that this can teach them to agree with false medical beliefs, and I designed a belief-grounded reward that also moves users toward the doctor-verified truth.
